YÚSHAB | THE BEAUTY EDIT

HOW TO OPEN
Extract the entire ZIP, then open Yushab-Preview.html. Alternatively open website/index.html directly. Keep the website folder and its assets together.

WHAT IS INCLUDED
152 real beauty, skincare and wellness product listings from the competitor's 157-item shop catalog. Five unrelated clothing listings were excluded. Products retain their original product/manufacturer branding. YÚSHAB is the retailer.

All 152 products have individual pages and locally included images. Seven variable face washes have 14 retrieved size/price variants. The catalog includes Brightening Creams, Skin Glow, Face Wash, Face Serum, Scrubs, Moisturizer, Supplements, Sunblock, Body Soap, Body & Wellness and Value Packs. Category groups overlap intentionally.

Browse with category/brand/price filters, text search, sorting and pagination. Add products and size variants to the shopping bag, change quantities and review a checkout preview. The bag is saved in your browser. Export a product selection or contact draft as a text file.

Pages include Home, Shop, 152 individual products, Brands A–Z, About Us, Contact Us, Shipment Policy, Payment Method, Refund & Return Policy, Privacy Policy, Terms & Conditions, FAQs, Follow US, Track an Order, Cart and Checkout Preview.

PRICES AND SOURCES
Prices match the competitor's catalog snapshot retrieved on 3 October 2026. Sale prices and variant ranges are preserved. This is a fixed snapshot, not a live price feed. Yushab-Catalog.csv documents every product, source page and image URL. Catalog-source.json preserves the detailed source records and variant data for future edits. Product images are sourced from the reference store and its image CDN. These are not generated YÚSHAB-manufactured products.

SHIPPING
Free shipping from Pakistan to Pakistan, Europe, the UK, USA, Canada and Australia follows the owner's instruction. Dispatch timelines, destination-specific availability and tax/import-charge handling require confirmation before live ordering.

EDITING
website/catalog.js contains the product data, website/app.js the interactions and website/style.css the visual design. All pages use local assets. No external fonts or runtime libraries are required.

UPDATE: CURRENCIES, PAYMENTS, DESIGN
website/config.js is the one file to edit: exchange rates, markup %, order email, WhatsApp number, card payment link, bank details and Western Union receiver details. Customers can switch between USD, GBP, EUR, CAD, AUD and PKR (auto-selected from browser language, saved afterwards). GBP/EUR/CAD/AUD refresh from a free ECB-based feed when online; PKR and offline fallbacks use the rates in config.js.
Checkout offers card, bank wire and Western Union. It creates an order reference and shows payment instructions. Nothing is sent automatically: the customer sends the order file by email/WhatsApp. Real card payments need a hosted payment link (Stripe, PayPal etc.) in config.js. The site never collects card numbers.
Fonts (Bodoni Moda, Figtree) are self-hosted in website/fonts. Colours are CSS variables at the top of style.css.
